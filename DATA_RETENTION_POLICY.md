# Data Retention Policy

This operational template requires legal review before launch.

- Active account and profile: while the account is active.
- Projects and tasks: until deleted, archived, or the account is deleted, subject to team and legal needs.
- Privacy requests and export metadata: long enough to resolve and evidence the request.
- Reports and moderation decisions: only as long as needed for safety, appeals, fraud prevention, disputes, and legal obligations.
- Security logs and audit logs: limited to the period needed for security investigation and legal obligations.
- Backups: rolling expiry; deleted data is removed as backups expire.

Automated deletion/anonymisation should run on a scheduled server-side job. Legal claims, fraud prevention, safety investigations, and legal obligations may require documented exceptions.
