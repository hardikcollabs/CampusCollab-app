# Community Guidelines

CampusCollab is for respectful student collaboration. No bullying, harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual content, hate speech, scams, plagiarism-for-hire, academic cheating services, malicious links, spam, identity misuse, or publishing someone else’s private information.

Represent your skills, availability, and project details honestly. Keep initial communication in-platform, avoid sharing sensitive information publicly, and use caution before meeting anyone offline.

Use the Report control for profiles or project posts. Reports include a reason and optional details and receive acknowledgement. Depending on severity, we may warn, remove content, restrict features, suspend, or terminate an account. Affected users receive a specific reason and can submit a free in-app appeal for review. We flag content for human review; we do not automatically remove content solely through AI.
